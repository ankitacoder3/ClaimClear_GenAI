## Notes
The room line cannot be checked: 22,000 for a 4-day stay = 5,500/day. If all 4 days were ward/room, cap 3,000/day -> excess 10,000 plus a proportionate deduction of 10,977 on non-medicine charges (Clause 4.1 Note 2), payable would be 46,097. If the days were ICU (procedure says ICU monitoring), ICU cap 7,500/day -> no excess, payable 66,025. The insurer's 3,000 (no clause) fits neither reading. Gold payable 66,025 is the figure with no unverifiable room deduction; the day-wise room/ICU break-up is needed. Also the letter applies co-pay before the room deduction (wrong order per DECISIONS #3). Treated List III items (gauze/cotton) as not separately payable even though there is no surgical procedure charge here.
