## Calculation

2. Less room/ICU rent above cap (cl. 4.1(i)/(ii)): 3,000
3. Less Annexure-A / excluded items (cl. 4.7, Annexure A; cl. 4.5 where noted): 650
4. Less ambulance above 2,000 (cl. 4.1.1(v)): 600
5. Proportionate deduction (cl. 4.1 Note 2; DECISIONS 1-2: only medicines exempt; lower ratio if both over): ratios room 2,000/3,000 = 0.666667; applied ratio 2/3 = 0.666667. Base (non-medicine hospital charges excluding room/ICU rent, non-payables, ambulance) = 53,500; deduction = 53,500 x (1 - 2/3) = 17,833
6. Admissible before sub-limit: 50,517
8. Co-pay 5% on admissible 50,517 (cl. 9.3; DECISIONS 3): 2,526
9. Net after co-pay: 47,991
10. Sum insured + CB remaining this policy year (cl. 2, 3.47): 1,00,000 (no earlier claims shown)
11. **Final payable: 47,991**
