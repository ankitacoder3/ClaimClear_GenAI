## 5. Proportionate deduction (Cl. 4.1 Note 2)
- Single room - room rent: ratio = cap / actual rate = 5/6 = 0.8333
- Ratio applied = 5/6 (0.8333).
- Base = all other hospital expenses except medicines (only medicines exempt, DECISIONS.md item 1), excluding non-payable/subsumed items and the ambulance benefit: Rs 99,850.
- Deduction = Rs 99,850 x (1 - 5/6) = Rs 16,642 (rounded half up).

## 6. Admissible amount
- Sum of admissible line amounts less proportionate deduction = Rs 1,15,658.

## 7. Co-payment (Cl. 9.3) and sum insured
- Co-pay 5% x Rs 1,15,658 = Rs 5,783 (rounded half up).
- Net after co-pay = Rs 1,09,875.
- No earlier claims this policy year; full Rs 3,00,000 available.
- Payable = min(net, SI remaining) = Rs 1,09,875.
