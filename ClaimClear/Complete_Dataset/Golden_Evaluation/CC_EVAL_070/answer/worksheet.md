## Evidence and reasoning

The road ambulance trip sheet confirms a medically indicated transfer to the admitting hospital. The separate ride receipt is for the patient's family after discharge, with no ambulance or medical service. Only the Rs 1,600 road ambulance charge enters the Rs 2,000 cap calculation.

The worksheet applies 5% co-payment to the admissible base where that amount can be established. See the case documents for the insurer calculation and the facts used to check it.
